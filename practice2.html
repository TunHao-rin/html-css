My Plan For DaLat GoGo 5.0

I have a plan to go to DaLat GoGo 5.0 with my friends. We will go there by bus and stay in a homestay for 3 days. We will visit some famous places in DaLat such as the Xuan Huong Lake, the Valley of Love, and the DaLat Market. We will also try some local food and take many photos. I am very excited about this trip and I hope everything goes well.

First day: We will arrive in DaLat in the morning and check in to our homestay. Then we will have breakfast in Banh Cuon Ong Si. In the noon, we will go to the cafe. In the afternoon, we will eat Lau Bo at Lau Bo Ba Toa Nha Go. After , we will go to DaLat Market to eat Banh Trang Nuong, Banh Roti, anh Sua Dau Nanh.
Second day: We will visit the Puppy Farm and Racing GoKart. In the noon, we will have lunch at a Tiem Com Hoi Do. In the afternoon, we will go to Cafe view in DaLat and take many photos. In the evening, we will have dinner at Tiem Nuong Thuong Thuong and then go back to our homestay and eat and drinking session.
Final day: We will Banh Uot Long Ga and then check out of our homestay. We will visit the Lam Vien Square and buy souvenirs. In the noon, we will eat Bun Bo and then go back to our bus station to return home.
