Manchester United

Football Club - Manchester, England

Manchester United is a professional football club based in Old Trafford, Greater Manchester, England. The club was founded in 1878 and competes in the Premier League, the top tier of English football.

Achievements of club

  • 20 English League titles
  • 12 FA Cups
  • 5 League Cups
  • 3 UEFA Champions League titles
  • 1 FIFA Club World Cup
